# PothOS by JoshOps AI

**Your world, continued.**

PothOS brings a continuing ArcheAgent, memory, files, tools and workflows into a
personal AI computing environment. It connects supported engines and devices
around your account, your work and the permissions you grant.

An **ArcheAgent** is a continuing digital representative with an identity and
responsibilities. A **champion** is the companion you designate to coordinate with
you. **Memex** is founder Joshua Gustafson-Juarez's champion; each person's
companion has its own identity. **PothOS Orb** names the companion interface.

The full product includes the interface, work and memory services, orchestration
and a local runtime on supported hardware. Available capabilities depend on the
device, installed release and connections enabled for that account.

## Start here

- [Sign in or create an account](https://pothos.joshops.ai).
- Check [current availability and setup](https://joshops.ai/docs).

Personal PothOS is **US$20 per independent ArcheAgent per month**. Supported
interfaces and Worlds are included; an additional independent ArcheAgent is a
separate subscription. Hardware, chosen hosting and AI-provider subscriptions
remain separate. See the [current pricing](https://joshops.ai/pricing).

Your account, subscriptions and connected services remain distinct. Connecting
Google, OpenAI or another supported service requires that service's authorization.
Another person's sign-in, memory or credentials are never included in your setup.

This public guide describes the product and its available entry points. It does
not publish the proprietary implementation or certify a future device capability.
