# PothOS and Purpose Link

**Current direction:** read the [PothOS community hub](../../POTHOS.md) and
[November 6 release-policy clarification](community-release-policy.md).
Prospective runtime terms are under review; existing documentation licenses remain effective.

[Purpose Link](https://purposelink.joshops.ai) is a separate, Houston-first
community resource navigation project in development. It gives the broader PothOS mission a practical context:
help people reach useful information, coordinate with appropriate providers
and pursue work that benefits their communities.

PothOS supplies the intended reusable foundation for people to create their
own ArcheAgents, maintain continuity and choose permitted tools. Purpose
Link's application, participants, records and organizational responsibilities
remain separate. A link between these projects is not a shared login, data
grant, partnership agreement or merger of their environments.

## Direction for collaboration

The goal is for communities in different cities and countries to adapt useful
tools to their own needs. Accessibility, language, local knowledge, responsible
resource updates and understandable consent are useful areas for contribution.
Worldwide adoption is an ambition, not a claim that a worldwide service or
deployment already exists.

Potential projects include helping a person keep track of resources they
chose, explaining how a resource was found, preserving corrections and
supporting a permitted handoff to a service provider. Each proposal needs
actual partner requirements and a clearly defined data boundary.

## Boundaries for a prototype

- Start with synthetic records and an explicitly selected community/use case.
- Keep personal and provider/organization environments separate.
- Let the person choose what, if anything, is sent to a provider.
- Do not copy private case files, family histories or application records into
  a public repository, demonstration or shared ArcheAgent.
- Record the source and observation date of resource information and expose
  uncertainty about its current availability.
- Treat referral, eligibility, professional advice and emergency response as
  distinct functions with their own real owners and requirements.
- Demonstrate the specific integration before describing it as operational.

Social justice and shared access to knowledge are project motivations. They
do not change the permissions required to access another person's data, and
they do not retroactively amend the existing documentation license.
