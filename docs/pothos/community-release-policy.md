# Community release policy

Updated November 6, 2026. This notice records the current publishing direction.
It is not a runtime software license.

## Purpose and commitment

PothOS is a free community project grounded in cognitive sovereignty and the
continuity work described in [Memex Continuity Lab](https://devpost.com/software/memex-continuity-lab).
Its purpose is to preserve meaning across changing infrastructure, platforms,
software, devices and models, reduce noise and help people recover useful signal
for their own development and service to others.

Official PothOS software is intended to remain free of charge. The founder
opposes charging people for PothOS and exploitation of their private cognitive
information. Prospective source-available runtime terms are being prepared.
Their treatment of related paid services and use in a person's own paid work
remains to be clarified before those terms are applied.

Engineering, privacy review and packaging continue. No new runtime or
developer-component license grant is being made while that scope is unresolved.

## Accurate licensing language

Restrictions on selling or commercial use do not meet the
[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd). Future restricted code
should be identified accurately as source-available under its actual terms.
The code has not yet been released here.

The [Apache license](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) grants
irrevocable copyright permissions subject to its conditions. Charging money
does not by itself breach that license. Earlier compliant recipients' rights
cannot simply be withdrawn by changing this announcement.

No universal remote revocation or shutdown of independently operated local
copies is promised. Project moderation, hosted-service control and valid
license termination are separate matters.

## Community expectations

The project rejects harvesting or selling private signal, deception, coercion,
unauthorized surveillance and exploitation. Public participation must respect
privacy, permission, source fidelity and honest evidence. Maintainers can
moderate the project spaces they actually control.

Voice and accessibility are immediate contribution priorities. The founder's
private environment and broad robotics control/hardware work are outside this
prepared release.

Start with the [PothOS community hub](../../POTHOS.md).
